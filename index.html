Features this prompt includes:

  • Most certainly better Dead Dove and Oneshots.
  • A pretty sarcastic, judging, and mocking narrator.
  • Negative bias—while unnecessary for my goat, some models do need a slap in their face to remind them about reality.
  • Clever implementation of onomatopoeia, fixing the 'they groaned' issue without screwing over sound description.
  • Multiple character responses in one response.

Recommended Models:

  • DeepSeek-V3-0324 (my goat) - The model that excels well in variant prose and quality. Only downside is its stupidity, but it's not as horrible as some might say.
  • DeepSeek-V3.2 - Still holds up well with this prompt. I like the outputs it gave me as it felt more grounded, but V3's just better for me.
  • Hy4 - I really like this model; it's definitely a balance between knowledge and prose!
  • Kimi K2-0905 and K2.6 - Really creative prose. I recommend to NOT use reasoning on Kimi models as they do overthink quite a lot... but they do also offer good quality.

Samplers I Use (for DeepSeek-V3-0324)

  • Temperature - 0.95. Trust me on this, I have not encountered a single word salad yet after 300 messages.
  • Top P - 0.96. Pretty basic.
  • Min P - 0.07. Yeah, I can't explain this well, it's pretty nerdy. (NOT ON J.AI)
  • Presence Penalty - 0.3. Prevents the model from repeating topics. (NOT ON J.AI)

Why even make this prompt?

This prompt was made to address my hatred of how generic my previous work was. This time, I decided to flip things around by NOT reinventing the same wheel again.

Why tell the model things it should already know? Why force it to follow the most boring prose rules? Why would a system prompt guide the clanker like a toddler? We are not in 2023 anymore—we have much smarter models to work with, and it baffles me how some prompts to this day (my old ones included) are still trying to micromanage them. No, Timmy... telling Claude to "make the prompt good, make no mistakes" isn't going to do wonders.

"Computer, don't use the words tapestry/delve/shivering," "Dickseek, don't use patterns x, y, z, a," "Clodi, no metagaming pls," "Computer, you are now roleplay master expert goonmaster." THOSE JUST DON'T WORK OR ARE TOO UNRELIABLE. (Unless you are using Fable 5.2 on xhigh reasoning, which means you are rich and stupid.)

Stop telling the clanker how to explain everything step-by-step. Just tell it what prose you are looking for, give it an example, add a few rules that actually address your model's clichés, and that's it! A prompt doesn't need 3,000 words to be good.

Samplers do much more than you think! Which is why it's such a shame that some frontends like Janitor don't have them included. Yeah, DeepSeek-V3-0324 isn't the gold standard when it comes to knowledge, but at least it's going to give me a good, different answer every reroll! It might be dumb, but at least it doesn't sound as stale and boring as the last 40 "state-of-the-art" models.
